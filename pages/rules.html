To Live or Die in Night City

Night City is a sprawling, dystopian metropolis where danger lurks around every corner. To survive—and even hrive—in this chaotic environment, there are some key principles and rules that can help you navigate its streets and underworld.Surviving in Night City requires a mix of caution, skill, and adaptability. Every choice you make has consequences, and the line between life and death is thin.

Information is power, so gather intel on your enemies and allies alike, while making sure you have enough eddies to buy your way out of tight situations. Lastly, never let your guard down—vigilance and a good understanding of Night City's tech and political landscape will give you the edge to not only survive but thrive. Here’s a list of rules to live (or die) by in Night City:

  1. Trust no one
    Night City is full of betrayal. From fixers to mercs, anyone can stab you in the back for eddies. Always be skeptical of motives, even from so-called "friends."

  2. Upgrade or Die
    Cyberware isn't just a luxury; it's essential for survival. Whether it's neural enhancements, reinforced limbs, or weapons, make sure you're always upgrading your body with the best tech.

  3. Keep a Low Profile—Unless You’re Ready for Heat
    You can go loud or quiet, but in Night City, drawing too much attention brings the police or powerful enemies. Sometimes it’s better to stay in the shadows than pick a fight you can’t win.

  4. Always Have an Exit Plan
    No matter how smooth a gig is going, things can go sideways fast. Always know how to get out—whether it’s through a hidden route or a backup plan for reinforcements.

  5. Choose Your Allies Wisely
    Gangs, fixers, corpos, and nomads all have their own agendas. Aligning with one group might protect you, but it will also make you enemies elsewhere. Choose carefully who you work with.

  6. Money Talks
    Eddies (the in-game currency) are power. Whether you're bribing a guard, upgrading cyberware, or paying for information, having money can open doors or save your life in a pinch.

  7. You’re Not Invincible
    No matter how powerful or well-equipped you are, there’s always someone bigger, stronger, or more connected. Stay humble, pick your battles, and avoid unnecessary risks.

  8. Live Fast, Die Hard
    Life expectancy is short in Night City. If you want to go out with a bang, live unapologetically and take risks—but remember, the bigger the risk, the bigger the fallout.
